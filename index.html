About the Program

Our mission: To ensure that every young player in our region has access to the equipment they need to play and develop — regardless of their family's financial situation.

Kick It Forward is a student-led community service program founded by two soccer players at Huron High School in Ann Arbor — Jonathan and Senith. We collect soccer equipment, including cleats, jerseys, shin guards, balls, goalkeeper gloves, portable goals, training cones, rebounders, and scoreboards, from clubs, schools, and individual donors. That equipment is then redistributed to underserved youth soccer programs and community leagues across the greater Ann Arbor and Detroit metro areas.

We run recurring seasonal collection drives throughout the year, steadily growing our network of donor clubs and partner programs so more players get equipped with every season.

What We Collect

We accept equipment in Good or Fair condition across these categories.

CategoryItems Accepted
Player gearCleats (all sizes), shin guards, goalkeeper gloves, socks
ApparelJerseys, shorts, training tops, warm-up jackets
Balls & goalsSoccer balls (all sizes), portable/pop-up goals, rebounders
Training equipmentCones, agility ladders, speed hurdles, training vests/bibs
Scoreboards & otherPortable scoreboards, corner flags, ball pumps, nets

Good

Clean, fully functional, minimal wear. Always accepted.

Fair

Functional but visibly worn. Accepted for high-need programs.

Poor

Broken, non-functional, or heavily damaged. Not accepted.
